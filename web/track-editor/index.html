    Meshes

      Control points

      Drag the numbered markers to reshape the loop. Shared points affect every loop that uses them; select one and use Make unique to diverge this loop. Release to rebuild. Hold Shift and click a marker to delete it.

      Guide lines

      Continuous light dotted lines show both kerb edges plus near and far prop lines on the outside and inside. Paint far lines with trees to place them behind the near barriers. Use the Prop button in the top menu to cycle what you paint. Click an empty guide to create a short segment, then drag its start or end handle along the spline. Click an existing segment to select it.

      Camera

      Use the View button to cycle front, back, left, right, and car cameras. In car view, arrow keys move along the spline. Drag the car marker in other views to reposition it.

      Drag empty space to orbit · right/shift-drag to pan · wheel or pinch to zoom

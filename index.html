Rust / Wasm / wgpu

Lapster

A web-native vehicle simulation engine and early drivable slice. Custom physics, deterministic asset packs, and a wgpu renderer with WebGPU and WebGL2.

Drive 500 Hz physics, chase camera, wheel loads, auto reverse. Garage Car on track with wheel steering and spin controls. Track Viewer Track render, axes, bounds, spawn, and collision surfaces. Debug Viewer Coordinate-frame sanity check: +Y up, -Z forward.
Controls: W/↑ throttle, S/↓ brake and auto reverse, A/D or ←/→ steer, R reset. Requires Chromium for WebGPU; WebGL2 is the fallback.
Source on GitHub
Car: Mazda RX-7 by IvOfficial, CC BY 3.0, via Poly Pizza. Track: Skidpad 0.5 (temporary demo).

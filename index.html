Rust / Wasm / wgpu

Lapster

A web-native vehicle simulation engine and early drivable slice. Custom physics, deterministic asset packs, and a wgpu renderer with WebGPU and WebGL2.

Drive 500 Hz physics, chase camera, wheel loads, auto reverse.
Skidpad 0.5 Drift Tracks
Garage Choose the car you drive; wheel controls and orbit camera. Track Viewer Track render, axes, bounds, spawn, and collision surfaces. Debug Viewer Coordinate-frame sanity check: +Y up, -Z forward.
Controls: W/↑ throttle, S/↓ brake and auto reverse, A/D or ←/→ steer, R reset. Requires Chromium for WebGPU; WebGL2 is the fallback.
